# Public specimen recognition study

This branch compiles and runs the macOS CLI from NumiAgent PR #20. The study compares fixed OCR, MobileNetV3-Small, DINOv2-Small and DINOv2-Base on the same inputs and catalog. The iOS and TestFlight workflows are separate. The corpus uses a larger study catalog to exercise visual matching across real specimens.

- Ancient coins use exact OCRE types with separate ANS accessions for each physical specimen. Source views come from the rights-cleared Syllogeum v1.1 index. The catalog selects pairs of types of the same issuer and denomination to include look-alikes. Other types are held out of the catalog. Images are public domain in the United States and credited to the American Numismatic Society. The ANS/Syllogeum source metadata and its derivative in `inputs.json` are available under [ODbL 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/). [Source and licenses](https://syllogeum.org/data#provenance)
- Modern coins are credited to Md Irfan Hossain under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Each potentially overlapping look-alike group contributes at most one specimen across all splits. Labels are issuer and denomination. Years and mint varieties are outside the modern subset's labels. [Source](https://huggingface.co/datasets/irfanhossainsust/multi-camera-world-coin-dataset/tree/3c14042bf841123f65d41733ffa7f28c232e8038)
- In-catalog banknotes use two published detail views each of genuine 500 and 1,000 taka notes with distinct JaalTaka specimen IDs. Denominations were visually confirmed from the archived reverse detail. Wear, stains and framing vary. The banknote experiment measures these two designs in this acquisition setup. The authors are Bibhas Roy Chowdhury Piyas, Fatama Jannat Tisha, Sadia Rahman, Shahrin Islam and Bijoy Roy Chowdhury Preenon. [JaalTaka v2, CC BY 4.0](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/2m7wk5cy4c/2)
- Out-of-catalog notes are distinct historic issue types from the Smithsonian National Numismatic Collection, National Museum of American History. The individual source images are marked public domain on Wikimedia Commons. [Source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:NNC_Confederate_States_of_America_Banknotes)

`inputs.json` freezes the specimen splits, catalog and image locations. The study saves up to two views per specimen, resizes to 1,280 pixels on the longest edge and removes embedded metadata. The original source hashes, final suite hash and all per-specimen predictions accompany the results. The corpus and stored input images are for the research run. Only device testing can establish installed app size, peak memory or latency on the target iPhone.

Use `python -m pip install pillow==13.0.0` and `python benchmarks/public-model-study/materialize.py SUITE` to reproduce the data preparation from the frozen manifest. Follow [the benchmark's Mac commands](../../ios/benchmark/README.md#convert-and-run) from model conversion onward, using that `SUITE` and keeping the generated `catalog.json`.
