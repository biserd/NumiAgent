## Description

Turn a tray of coins or an album of banknotes into a private catalog you can search.

- Capture photos with your iPhone or select them from your library. Keep drafts on your device until you’re ready to upload.
- Review optional AI-assisted identification suggestions and keep your records, photos, and references together.
- Compare actual sold lots and see a price range when enough comparable sales support one.
- Track which pieces you own and what’s missing from your collection checklists.

Your iPhone and web catalog use the same NumiAgent account. Explore the demo or sign in with an email link to open your collection.
