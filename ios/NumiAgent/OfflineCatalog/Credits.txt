The expanded database includes OCRE coin types published by the American Numismatic Society and the Institute for the Study of the Ancient World, from the archived 2019-12-04 Nomisma extract maintained by Neel Smith. The OCRE data is available under ODbL 1.0.

Modern coins and banknotes
Issue facts and inscriptions are credited to the U.S. Mint, the U.S. Currency Education Program, the Royal Canadian Mint, the Bank of Canada Museum, the Hong Kong Monetary Authority, and the European Central Bank. Each entry links to its source.
The expanded U.S. quarter designs are credited to the U.S. Mint’s State Quarters, D.C. and U.S. Territories, America the Beautiful, and American Women Quarters program records.

Historic banknotes
Type facts are transcribed from public-domain Wikimedia Commons records, including photographs from the National Numismatic Collection at the Smithsonian National Museum of American History. Each entry links to its source and credit.
Additional banknote records come from the Smithsonian National Numismatic Collection. Their Open Access metadata is CC0.
